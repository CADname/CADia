Create a rigid flange coupling assembly for connecting two Ø20 mm shafts. Model two separate coupling halves. Each half should have a hub with an outer diameter of 40 mm and a length of 35 mm, connected to a flange with an outer diameter of 100 mm and a thickness of 12 mm. Create a Ø20 mm shaft bore through each hub and add a 6 mm wide keyway. Place four Ø10 mm bolt holes equally spaced on a 70 mm pitch circle diameter. Assemble the two flange halves face-to-face with their bolt holes aligned.
