Create a functional universal joint assembly for connecting two Ø15 mm shafts at an angle. Model two separate fork-shaped yokes, each with a hub outer diameter of approximately 35 mm and a Ø15 mm shaft bore. Create a central cross-shaped spider with four cylindrical trunnions of Ø10 mm. Assemble the two yokes at 90 degrees to each other and connect them through the spider so that the two shafts can rotate while allowing angular misalignment. Use appropriate clearances between the trunnions and yoke holes.
