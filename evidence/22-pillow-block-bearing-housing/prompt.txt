Create a single-solid pillow block bearing housing for a 6205 bearing. Make a rectangular base plate 120 mm long, 60 mm wide, and 12 mm thick. Add two vertical mounting holes of Ø10 mm through the base, positioned symmetrically 45 mm to the left and right of the center. Create a central cylindrical bearing housing with an outer diameter of 70 mm and a width of 32 mm. Create a concentric through bore of Ø52 mm for the bearing seat. Connect the cylindrical housing to the base using two symmetric triangular support ribs, each 8 mm thick. Add a 5 mm fillet to the outer edges where the cylindrical housing and support ribs meet the base. Add a centered lubrication hole of Ø6 mm from the top of the cylindrical housing through to the bearing bore. The final model must be one continuous solid body.
