Create a three-blade mechanical propeller with an overall diameter of approximately 160 mm. Create a central hub with an outer diameter of 40 mm, a length of 30 mm, and a Ø15 mm shaft bore. Add three equally spaced blades around the hub. Each blade should taper from approximately 28 mm wide at the root to 14 mm near the tip and include a gradual twist from root to tip. Apply smooth transitions and fillets between the blades and hub.
